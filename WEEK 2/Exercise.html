Blue Cupcake:

Prep Time:15 minutes | Cook Time: 12 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup chocolate

Instructions:

Mix all ingredients together. Drop spoonfuls on baking sheet. Bake at 350 F for 12 minutes until golden brown.

Difficulty: Easy
